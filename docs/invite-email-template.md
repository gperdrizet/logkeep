WHAT IS LOGKEEP?

LogKeep helps you save and organize interesting links you find online. Think of it as your personal library - when you come across an article, tutorial, or webpage you want to remember, just submit the link and LogKeep will:

  • Save it to your personal collection
  • Extract the title automatically
  • Generate a quick summary (so you remember why you saved it)
  • Organize everything in one searchable place
